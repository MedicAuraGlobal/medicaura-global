Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

MedicAura Global ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and professional information of nurses, doctors, and healthcare professionals using our mobile application and website (medicauraglobal.com).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and professional profile details when you register.
  • Document Vault Files: Healthcare licenses, certificates (such as ACLS, BLS, PHTLS), and expiry dates that you voluntarily upload to receive expiry alerts.
  • App Usage Data: Saved jobs, language preferences, and AI eligibility queries to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide verified international healthcare job listings and direct application links.
  • To send automated 60-day and 7-day document expiry reminders.
  • To evaluate job eligibility using our AI assistant when requested by you.
  • To maintain account security and provide customer support.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account data and uploaded documents are securely stored using encrypted cloud infrastructure (Supabase). We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or uploaded documents to third-party recruiters or marketing agencies.

4. Third-Party Links

When you click "Apply on Official Website" for a job vacancy, you are redirected to the respective hospital or official portal. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can update or delete your uploaded documents anytime inside the app. To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at support@medicauraglobal.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@medicauraglobal.com
